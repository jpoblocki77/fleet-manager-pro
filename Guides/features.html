Getting around

Menu and page help

Where: Left side of every page · Who: Everyone

What it does

The menu groups every page under Operations, Company, Community and Admin. Each page opens with a How to use this page panel with short steps that fit your role.

How to use it

  1. Click a page in the menu on the left.
  2. Read How to use this page at the top.
  3. Fold the panel away when you know the page. Your browser remembers that for each page.

Good to know

  • Admin pages only show for Managers and the Owner.
  • Click a driver, a job or a truck on most pages to see its details.

Game box (ATS, ETS2 or both)

Where: Top bar · Who: Everyone

What it does

Chooses which game's data you see and in which units: ATS in miles and pounds, ETS2 in kilometres and tonnes, or both games.

How to use it

  1. Open the Game box at the top.
  2. Pick ATS only, ETS2 only or Both games.

Good to know

  • It filters the logbook, dashboard, dispatch, events, leaderboard, driver figures, garages, trucks and map.
  • Money (Finance) is for the whole company.
  • If jobs seem to be missing, check this box first.

Connection status

Where: Top bar · Who: Everyone

What it does

Shows whether Fleet Manager Pro can see your game, and your truck and speed while you drive.

How to use it

  1. Start the game and get into your truck.
  2. Look at the top bar: your speed and truck appear.

Good to know

  • waiting for game: the game plugin isn't loaded. Install it in Settings > Telemetry.
  • game not running: the game is closed or still in its menus.

Notifications

Where: Bell in the top bar · Who: Everyone

What it does

Tells you about things meant for you: a contract assigned to you, a level up, low fuel, an event that changed, a new application.

How to use it

  1. Click the bell. The red number counts unread ones.
  2. Click a notification to open what it is about.
  3. Click x to delete one, Mark all read to clear the number, or Delete all.

Light and dark look

Where: Sun button in the top bar · Who: Everyone

What it does

Switches between the dark and light look.

How to use it

  1. Click the sun button. Click it again to go back.

Good to know

  • Company colours are a separate setting: see Company settings.

Roles

Where: Drivers · Who: Managers and the Owner set them

What it does

A member's role decides what they can do: Owner (everything), Manager (fleet, finance, members, recruitment and settings), Dispatcher (contracts, events and announcements), Driver and Trainee (drive, claim contracts, log jobs, sign up for events).

How to use it

  1. Open Drivers and click the member.
  2. Under Management, choose the role and press Save.

Good to know

  • Only the Owner can make someone a Manager, take loans, or dissolve the company.
  • A Manager can't edit or remove anyone at their own level or above.

While you drive

Dashboard

Where: Operations > Dashboard · Who: Everyone

What it does

Your company at a glance: the last 14 days of revenue and distance, who is driving now, recent jobs, top drivers, your wallet and level, news, upcoming events and open contracts.

How to use it

  1. Open it when you start a session.
  2. Look at Open contracts for work and Announcements for news.
  3. Click any job, driver or event to open it.

Good to know

  • Managers also see jobs awaiting approval and new applications.

Live Cockpit

Where: Operations > Live Cockpit · Who: Everyone

What it does

A live dashboard for your truck that updates 4 times a second: speed and limit, RPM, gear, cruise control, current job, fluids, damage, your pedals and steering, lights, warnings, navigation, Hours of Service, trailers and wheels.

How to use it

  1. Start the game and get into your truck. The page fills in by itself.
  2. Put it on a second screen, or on a tablet with Connect a Tablet.
  3. Press Stream overlay for OBS, or All data points for every value.

Fuel & AdBlue

Where: Operations > Fuel & AdBlue · Who: Everyone

What it does

Live tank levels and a graph of them over time, the diesel price in the state you are in, what filling up would cost, and a list of every fill-up with its price and cost.

How to use it

  1. Drive: the bars and graph fill in by themselves.
  2. Pick 1 hour, 6 hours, 24 hours or 7 days.
  3. Scroll down for the fill-ups.

Good to know

  • Diesel is charged at the game's own price in each state or country.
  • The games have no AdBlue price: set yours in Settings > Economy.
  • Readings are kept for 30 days.
  • Managers can pick another driver at the top.
  • The driver, Managers and the Owner are told when fuel or AdBlue runs low. Turn that off in Settings > Compliance.

Fleet Map

Where: Operations > Fleet Map · Who: Everyone

What it does

A real map of borders and cities. Roads your company drives are drawn in and remembered, places you visit are marked, and drivers on the road show live.

How to use it

  1. Scroll to zoom and drag to move.
  2. Choose ATS or ETS2.
  3. Press Whole map to see everything, or Fit drivers to zoom to the trucks driving now.

Good to know

  • The map starts empty and fills in as your drivers haul.
  • Cities become exact once you have been there; unvisited UK cities in ETS2 are approximate.

Telemetry Data

Where: Operations > Telemetry Data · Who: Everyone

What it does

Every value the game sends, grouped and searchable: truck, engine, lights, damage, job, navigation, wheels and trailers. Values that change flash red.

How to use it

  1. Type part of a name to find a value, or pick a group.
  2. Press Pause to hold the list still.
  3. Press Download snapshot to save everything as a JSON file.

Good to know

  • Managers also get a raw view for troubleshooting.

In-game overlay

Where: Operations > Overlays · Who: Everyone

What it does

A small panel that floats over the game with the values you choose, plus pop-ups for breaks due, drive time running out, violations, inspections, defects and low fuel.

How to use it

  1. Run the game in windowed or borderless mode.
  2. Tick the values you want: driving, job, fuel and truck, or Hours of Service.
  3. Choose alerts, position, opacity, size and the hide/show key.
  4. Press Save, then Open the overlay.
  5. In the game, press Ctrl + I (or your key) to hide or show it.

Good to know

  • Clicks go straight through it to the game.
  • Exclusive full screen hides it, like every other window.
  • It can open by itself when Fleet Manager Pro starts, and hide when the game is closed.

Stream overlay (OBS)

Where: Operations > Overlays · Who: Everyone

What it does

A see-through panel for OBS, a second screen or a tablet: company and driver, speed, gear, trip, deadline, fuel, Hours of Service, damage, clock, your chosen values and alerts.

How to use it

  1. Choose a layout: wide bar, column or compact.
  2. Tick what to show, set the size and watch the preview.
  3. Press Copy to copy your link.
  4. In OBS: Sources > + > Browser, paste the link, width 1920, height 220, OK.
  5. Drag it where you want it.

Good to know

  • Keep the link private: it signs in by itself. If it leaks, press New token on your profile.
  • Use Solid background for a second screen; leave it off for OBS.

Connect a Tablet

Where: Community > Connect a Tablet · Who: Everyone; Managers and the Owner turn it on

What it does

Opens Fleet Manager Pro on a tablet or phone by scanning a code. You are signed in as yourself.

How to use it

  1. A Manager presses Turn on tablet access. If Windows asks, choose Private networks and Allow.
  2. Put the tablet on the same Wi-Fi as the PC.
  3. Scan the code with the camera and tap the link.
  4. The browser warns about the certificate the first time. iPad: Show Details > visit this website. Android: Advanced > Proceed.
  5. Use Add to Home Screen to open it like an app.

Good to know

  • Each code works once and for a few minutes. Keep it private.
  • Press Turn off tablet access when you don't need it.

Jobs and dispatch

Logbook

Where: Operations > Logbook · Who: Everyone

What it does

Every delivery. A job you drive is logged the moment you deliver it, with route, cargo, distance, pay, costs, profit and driving score.

How to use it

  1. Start Fleet Manager Pro before you drive.
  2. Take a job in the game and deliver it.
  3. Use search, driver, status and game, then Filter.
  4. Click a job to open it.
  5. Press Export CSV for a spreadsheet.

Good to know

  • Jobs from the demo simulator are marked demo and can be cleared.

Job details

Where: Logbook > click a job · Who: Everyone; Managers and the Owner approve and delete

What it does

One delivery in full: the route you drove, a timeline, every cost, anything the game took off the pay, damage, speeding, fuel and the driving score.

How to use it

  1. Open a job from the Logbook.
  2. Managers: press Approve & pay or Reject on a held job, or Clear flags.
  3. Managers: Delete job removes it.

Good to know

  • Pay is held when the checks find something odd: a position jump without a ferry or train, too much distance, a trip finished too fast, a drive the app barely saw, or pay far above the offer.

Log a job by hand

Where: Logbook > Log a job · Who: Everyone

What it does

Adds a delivery you drove without telemetry.

How to use it

  1. Press Log a job.
  2. Fill in the cargo, route, distance and pay. Add fuel, tolls, fines and damage if you know them.
  3. Put a screenshot link in Notes / proof if your company wants one.
  4. Press Submit job.

Good to know

  • Drivers' manual jobs wait for a Manager unless that is turned off in Settings > Economy.

Driving score, XP, levels and ranks

Where: Logbook and your profile · Who: Everyone

What it does

Each delivery gets a score from 0 to 100. You start at 100 and lose points for cargo damage, truck wear, fines, speeding and arriving late. Distance and weight earn XP, and a better score earns more.

How to use it

  1. Drive carefully and on time.
  2. See a job's score on its page.
  3. See your level and next rank on your profile.

Good to know

  • The most you can lose: cargo damage 40, fines 30 (8 each), truck wear 25, speeding 25, late 15.
  • Special transport adds 25% of the base XP and a company contract adds 20%.
  • Ranks: Rookie, Driver (level 5), Senior Driver (10), Road Captain (20), Veteran (35), Elite (50), Legend (75).
  • XP rates and how much speeding is forgiven are in Settings > Economy.

Dispatch Board

Where: Operations > Dispatch · Who: Everyone can claim; Dispatchers, Managers and the Owner post

What it does

Contracts the company wants driven, with a bonus, priority and deadline. A matching delivery completes the contract by itself.

How to use it

  1. Dispatchers: press New contract.
  2. Choose the game, cities, companies and cargo from the lists. Only the destination city is required.
  3. Set the bonus, deadline, priority and who it is for, then Post contract.
  4. Drivers: press Claim. Release gives it back.
  5. Pick up the matching load in the game and deliver it.

Good to know

  • Company lists only show companies that are in the city you picked.
  • It matches even when the driver plays in another language.
  • Dispatchers can press Done, Cancel or Reopen.
  • You can't claim when you are out of hours or your truck is on hold.

Send to game

Where: Dispatch > Send to game · Who: Everyone, in a browser on the game PC

What it does

Puts a contract into the game's freight market so you can pick it up in the truck.

How to use it

  1. Save your game.
  2. Press Send to game on the contract and pick the save to start from.
  3. In the game, load Fleet Manager Pro - Contract #N.
  4. Drive to the pickup company, take the job and deliver it.

Good to know

  • It makes a new save and never changes your others.
  • The contract needs a From city, To city and Cargo from the lists.
  • Single-player only: TruckersMP doesn't use your saves.
  • The job stays in the market for about a game day.

Route Planner

Where: Company > Route Planner · Who: Everyone

What it does

Tells you whether a lane pays before you take it, using your company's own deliveries: average pay, costs, profit, time on the road and where to go next.

How to use it

  1. Choose a From city and a To city. Cargo is optional.
  2. Press Check this lane.
  3. New lane? Type the distance to get an estimate from your averages.
  4. Look at Best next legs and Most profitable lanes.

Good to know

  • It needs some deliveries before it has anything to show.

ELD & Compliance

Electronic logbook (Hours of Service)

Where: Operations > ELD & Compliance · Who: Everyone

What it does

Records your duty status on in-game time and shows how long you can still drive. ATS uses US rules and ETS2 uses EU rules, each with its own log.

How to use it

  1. Drive: driving and on-duty time record themselves. Sleeping in the game logs as sleeper berth.
  2. Press Off duty, Sleeper or On duty when you stop for the day.
  3. Use Previous day and Next day to look back.

Good to know

  • US: 11 h driving, 14 h window, 30-minute break after 8 h, 70 h in 8 days or 60 h in 7.
  • EU: 9 h driving, 45-minute break after 4.5 h, 56 h a week.
  • Breaking a rule is a violation with a fine.

Simulate rest

Where: ELD & Compliance · Who: Each driver, for themselves

What it does

Takes a break, a sleeper berth or a full restart now, without waiting in the game.

How to use it

  1. Press the rest you need: break, sleeper berth or restart.
  2. Your clocks update and the rest shows in green on the graph.

Good to know

  • Only you can simulate your own rest.

Driver bank accounts

Where: Operations > My Bank · Who: Everyone for themselves; Managers and the Owner see the drivers they manage

What it does

Each driver has a bank account of their own, separate from the company account. Pay is deposited every Friday at noon.

How to use it

  1. Deliver loads: the pay shows under Next payday until Friday.
  2. Fines, tickets and licence fines come out of it. Open Statement to see each one.
  3. Open a Pay slip to see the loads in it.

Good to know

  • Every account opens with 3,000; change it in Settings > Economy.
  • Noon is the clock of the PC Fleet Manager Pro runs on. If it was closed, the payday is made up when it next runs.
  • The company pays at delivery, so the week's payroll is always set aside.

Personal loans

Where: Operations > My Bank > Personal loans · Who: Everyone for themselves

What it does

Borrow at one of four APRs. The money goes straight into your own bank account and is paid back from it every Friday.

How to use it

  1. Press Take a loan.
  2. Pick a plan, type the amount and look at the weekly payment.
  3. Press Borrow. Press Pay off now any time.

Good to know

  • Short 4 weeks 4.9%, Starter 12 weeks 6.9%, Standard 26 weeks 8.9%, Extended 52 weeks 11.5%.
  • 500 to 25,000, up to three loans at once.
  • They work like the company's loans but never touch the company's money. They can be turned off in Settings > Economy.

Driver's licences: CDL & Points

Where: Operations > CDL & Points · Who: Everyone for themselves; Managers and the Owner see all

What it does

Drivers pass a drivers test of two in-game hours to get a licence with 12 points. Breaking the law costs points and a ticket.

How to use it

  1. Managers: turn it on in Settings > Compliance. Issue licences to all current drivers skips the test for your existing team.
  2. Drivers: sit in your truck with the game running and press Start the drivers test.
  3. Work through pre-trip, control skills, the road drive and parking. The page shows your marks live; 80 of 100 pass.
  4. At zero points the licence is suspended: wait, or press Pay to clear your points, then retake the test.

Good to know

  • One point comes back every real day.
  • Every 5 real minutes of speeding costs a point.
  • Without a licence a driver can't claim contracts, and new loads are held until a manager approves them.
  • Off until you turn it on.

Vehicle inspections (DVIR)

Where: ELD & Compliance > DVIR · Who: Everyone; Managers and the Owner mark repairs

What it does

A pre-trip or post-trip report of eight items. Telemetry hints help you spot problems.

How to use it

  1. Choose pre-trip or post-trip and your truck.
  2. Mark each item OK or Defect and add notes.
  3. Tick the signature box and press Submit report.
  4. Managers: mark the report Repaired when it is fixed.

Good to know

  • Picking up a load without a pre-trip in the last 24 game-hours is a violation.
  • A defect puts the truck on hold until it is repaired, and repairs cost a set price per defect.

Roadside inspections

Where: ELD & Compliance > Inspections · Who: Everyone sees them; Dispatchers and above can start one

What it does

On some loads an inspector checks you at a stop on the way. Level 1 checks driver, vehicle and load; level 2 driver and vehicle; level 3 hours and reports.

How to use it

  1. Keep your hours legal, do your pre-trip and fix defects.
  2. Read the results and pass rate here.
  3. Dispatchers: pick a driver who is driving and a level to inspect them now.

Good to know

  • Serious findings put you out of service with a bigger fine.
  • The chance per load starts at 15%. Change it in Settings > Compliance.

Reset compliance records

Where: ELD & Compliance > Reset · Who: Managers and the Owner

What it does

Clears logbooks, violations, reports or inspections for one driver or all, for example for a new season.

How to use it

  1. Press Reset.
  2. Choose the driver, game and what to clear.
  3. Type RESET and confirm.

Good to know

  • It can't be undone. Fines already charged stay in the books.

Trucks, trailers and leasing

Trucks

Where: Company > Fleet & Garages · Who: Everyone can look; Managers and the Owner make changes

What it does

Your trucks with plate, driver, garage, odometer, condition and jobs. A truck is added by itself the first time it is driven.

How to use it

  1. Drive, and the truck appears. Or press Truck to buy one from the dealer list.
  2. Click a truck for its condition, jobs and maintenance.
  3. Press Edit to change its plate, driver, garage or service interval.
  4. Press Sell to take it out of the fleet and bank the money.
  5. Delete sold clears sold trucks from the list.

Good to know

  • A purchase can't take the company account below zero.
  • Suggested resale is 60% of its value.
  • A leased truck can't be sold: buy it out first.

Maintenance

Where: Fleet & Garages > Maintenance log > Record · Who: Everyone can look; Managers and the Owner make changes

What it does

Records services, repairs, tyres, inspections, washes and upgrades. Repairs you do in the game are noticed by themselves.

How to use it

  1. Press Record, pick the truck and the type.
  2. Check the cost, add notes and press Save.

Good to know

  • Prices come from Settings > Economy and can't be changed here. A repair costs the truck's wear times the repair rate.
  • A service clears the service due reminder.

Garages

Where: Company > Fleet & Garages · Who: Everyone can look; Managers and the Owner make changes

What it does

Home bases for your trucks, in any garage location in either game.

How to use it

  1. Press Garage and choose a location and size.
  2. Leave the price blank for the standard price, then Buy garage.
  3. Use Edit to change it, or Delete to sell it.

Good to know

  • Small: 1 truck, 100,000, 500 a week. Medium: 3 trucks, 250,000, 1,500 a week. Large: 5 trucks, 450,000, 2,500 a week.

Trailers

Where: Company > Fleet & Garages · Who: Everyone can look; Managers and the Owner make changes

What it does

Trailers the company owns or leases.

How to use it

  1. Press Trailer and choose a model: name, brand and price fill in.
  2. Set its type, plate and garage, then Add trailer.
  3. Press Sell or Delete when you no longer need it.

Good to know

  • Suggested resale is 60% of what you paid.

Truck Leasing

Where: Company > Truck Leasing · Who: Everyone can look; Managers and the Owner make changes

What it does

Use a truck for a small down payment and a weekly payment, with a set number of miles included. Every cost is shown before you sign.

How to use it

  1. Pick a new truck, or one you own, under Compare lease plans.
  2. Compare length, miles, interest, down payment, weekly payment and buyout price.
  3. Press Lease on under the plan you want.
  4. Click the lease to see miles used, your pace and what ending it today would cost.
  5. After the last payment: Buy it, Return or Renew.

Good to know

  • Payments are taken from the company account every week.
  • Built-in plans: Short Haul (12 weeks), Regional (26), Long Haul (52), Owner-Operator (104).
  • Extra miles are charged when you return it. Ending early costs a share of the payments left.
  • Managers add or change plans with New plan and Edit; signed leases keep their terms.

Trailer Leasing

Where: Company > Trailer Leasing · Who: Everyone can look; Managers and the Owner make changes

What it does

The same idea for trailers, with no mileage limit.

How to use it

  1. Pick a trailer under Compare trailer lease plans.
  2. Compare the plans and press Lease on.
  3. Click a lease to see what it costs and what ending it would cost.
  4. At the end: buy it, return it at no charge, or renew.

Good to know

  • Built-in plans: Short Term (12 weeks), Seasonal (26), Annual (52), Long Term (104).
  • The game doesn't report trailer mileage, so there is no mileage charge.

Money and results

Finance

Where: Company > Finance · Who: Everyone can look; Managers and the Owner make changes

What it does

The company ledger: freight income, driver pay, fuel, tolls, fines, repairs, garages, leases and loans, with a 30-day cash-flow chart.

How to use it

  1. Read the balance, income and expenses at the top.
  2. Look at By category and Driver earnings.
  3. Managers: press Entry to record money in or out by hand.
  4. Press CSV for a spreadsheet.

Good to know

  • Every company starts with 300,000. That can't be changed.
  • Costs from driving are booked by themselves.

Loans

Where: Finance > Take a loan · Who: The Owner

What it does

Borrows money for the company and pays it back every week.

How to use it

  1. Press Take a loan.
  2. Choose a plan: Starter (12 weeks, 6.9%), Standard (26 weeks, 8.9%) or Expansion (52 weeks, 11.5%).
  3. Type an amount from 10,000 to 5,000,000 and press Borrow.
  4. Press Pay off now to finish early.

Good to know

  • Up to 3 loans at a time. There is no penalty for paying off early.

Expenses

Where: Company > Expenses · Who: Everyone can look; Managers and the Owner make changes

What it does

Every cost the company has paid, with search, filters and totals by category.

How to use it

  1. Filter by category, truck, driver, source or words.
  2. Managers: press Add expense for costs the game doesn't know, such as insurance or parking.
  3. Use Edit or Delete to fix an expense entered by hand.

Good to know

  • The page shows the newest 500. Press CSV for all of them.

Insights

Where: Company > Insights · Who: Everyone

What it does

How the company really makes money: profit margin, pay, cost and profit per mile or kilometre, 12-month trends, and the best lanes, cargo, trucks and drivers.

How to use it

  1. Pick a period at the top.
  2. Compare profit with cost per distance.
  3. Use the best lanes and cargo to choose your next loads.

Good to know

  • Only delivered loads count.

Driver Performance

Where: Company > Driver Performance · Who: Everyone

What it does

Each driver's loads, distance, on-time rate, score, revenue, damage and fines.

How to use it

  1. Pick a period.
  2. Click a driver to see each load's deadline and arrival time.

Drivers and recruitment

Drivers and divisions

Where: Company > Drivers · Who: Everyone can look; Managers and the Owner make changes

What it does

Everyone in the company: role, division, level, jobs, distance, hours left and who is on the road.

How to use it

  1. Press Add member, type a username and temporary password, choose a role and press Add.
  2. Give the driver their username and password.
  3. Press Divisions to make teams, then set a member's division on their profile.

Good to know

  • Passwords need at least 8 characters.

Profiles

Where: Drivers > click a member · Who: Everyone edits their own; Managers and the Owner manage others

What it does

A member's level, recent jobs, favourite cargo, destinations, achievements, records and contact names.

How to use it

  1. Open your own profile to change your name, colour, country, bio or password.
  2. Managers: change role, status, division or pay, or reset a password.
  3. New token makes a fresh key for remote telemetry and the stream overlay.

Good to know

  • Leave the company deletes your account for good.
  • Fire (Managers) deletes a member for good. Deliveries and pay stay in the books as "Former driver".

Connect a driver (their own PC)

Where: Drivers > Connect a driver · Who: Managers and the Owner

What it does

Lets a driver play on their own PC, at home or anywhere, and have their jobs logged here.

How to use it

  1. Press Let other PCs in (once).
  2. Save your internet address, or press Find it for me.
  3. Press Open the port for me, or forward the port on your router yourself.
  4. Pick the driver and press Download file (or Copy the line). Send it only to them.
  5. The driver installs Fleet Manager Pro and the game plugin, puts the file in their Fleet Manager Pro folder, double-clicks it and leaves it open.

Good to know

  • The driver needs no key or company of their own.
  • The file holds their private token. If it leaks, make a New token and send a new file.
  • Keep your PC on, with Fleet Manager Pro open, while they drive.
  • Drivers on your own Wi-Fi don't need the router step.

Let other PCs in (LAN mode)

Where: Drivers > Connect a driver · Who: Managers and the Owner

What it does

Lets other PCs on your network open your company in a browser, encrypted.

How to use it

  1. Press Let other PCs in. It works at once and at every start.
  2. Allow it if Windows Firewall asks.
  3. On the other PC, open the https:// address the page shows.
  4. Accept the certificate warning the first time, then sign in.

Good to know

  • While it is off (and tablet access is too), only your own PC can open it.
  • Stop letting other PCs in disconnects everyone.
  • Starting with Fleet Manager Pro (LAN) still works, but you don't need it.

Recruitment

Where: Company > Recruitment · Who: Managers and the Owner

What it does

An online Apply page for your company. It works even when your PC is off, and applications arrive here.

How to use it

  1. Copy your Apply link and share it on Discord, TruckersMP or your website.
  2. Press Check now, or wait about 10 minutes.
  3. Choose Accept, Interview or Reject.
  4. Accepting makes the driver's account: send them their username and password.

Good to know

  • Open or close applications and change the page's text in Settings > Recruitment.
  • Clean up old applications deletes ones you have already decided.
  • At most 300 can wait at once, so open the app regularly.
  • A blue New application banner shows on every page until you review it or press Hide.

VTC Profile

Where: Company > VTC Profile · Who: Managers and the Owner

What it does

Your company's public page in the VTC Finder, where drivers look for a company to join.

How to use it

  1. Write about your company and choose its game, region, style and languages.
  2. Say what you ask for and offer, and add links and up to 5 questions of your own.
  3. Tick List my company in the VTC Finder and press Save.

Good to know

  • Not listed until you tick the box. Untick and save to take it off at once.
  • Names, money and your PC's address are never shown.
  • Answers to your questions show on the Recruitment page, with where the driver found you.

Global Leaderboard

Where: Leaderboard > Global · Who: Everyone can look; Managers and the Owner choose what is shared

What it does

Ranks every Fleet Manager Pro company together, and the drivers of companies that share their names. A public page shows the same board.

How to use it

  1. Press Global on the Leaderboard.
  2. Pick what to rank by, the period and the game.
  3. Press Update now to refresh, at most once every 30 seconds.
  4. Managers: untick Show my company on the global leaderboard to leave, or tick Show my drivers' names too.

Good to know

  • It updates every 10 minutes while the app runs.
  • Shared: your company name, currency and totals, including revenue. Driver names only if you tick the box.
  • Leaving removes your company straight away, and so does dissolving the company.
  • Simulator drives don't count. Companies that don't update for 30 days drop off.
  • Each company reports its own numbers, so treat it as friendly competition.
