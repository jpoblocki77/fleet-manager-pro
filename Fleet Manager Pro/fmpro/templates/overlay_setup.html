Your overlay link

This link signs in by itself with your personal key, so OBS doesn't need your password. Keep it private: anyone with it can see your live truck data. If it leaks, make a new key on your profile (New token).

Add it to OBS

  1. In OBS, under Sources, press + and choose Browser.
  2. Paste the link into URL. Set Width 1920 and Height 220.
  3. Press OK, then drag the overlay where you want it on your scene.

OBS on another PC: use this PC's network address instead of 127.0.0.1 (Connect a Tablet shows it).
