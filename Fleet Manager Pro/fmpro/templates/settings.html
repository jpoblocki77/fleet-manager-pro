Moving to another PC

Each purchase works on 2 PCs. To move, click Deactivate this PC here, then activate on the new PC with the same key. If the old PC is gone (broken, reinstalled, sold), ask the seller to release it.

Your company data lives in the data folder — copy it to the new PC to keep everything.

Dissolve the company

Ends the VTC. Every member except you leaves for good, as if fired: their accounts and personal records are deleted and their connections from their own PCs stop and close by themselves. Recruiting closes, and if Fleet Manager Pro opened a port on your router, it's closed again. Your internet address and extra host names are forgotten.

Choose whether to keep your own books (jobs, money, fleet; past deliveries show as "Former driver") or erase everything too. Erasing saves a backup to data\backups first.
