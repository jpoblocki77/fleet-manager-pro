The drivers test

Two in-game hours in your own truck, marked from live telemetry. You start with 100 marks and need {{ T.PASS }}.

  1. Pre-trip: brakes, air, lights, signals, hazards, wipers, condition and fuel.
  2. Control: couple a trailer, back up straight, back up round a bend, one smooth stop. Parts 1 and 2 share the first hour.
  3. Road: drive until the two hours are up, with at least {{ T.MIN_MOVING }} in-game minutes on the move.
  4. Park: stop, set the parking brake, engine off.

Marks come off for speeding, harsh braking, fast bends, no headlights in the dark, turns without a signal and fines. An accident, a red light, wrong-way driving, sleeping, ferries, loading a save or changing truck ends the test.
