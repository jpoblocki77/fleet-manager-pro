What it is

One app instead of a dozen spreadsheets, bots and websites.

Running a virtual trucking company usually means juggling a job tracker, a Discord bot, a spreadsheet for money, a recruitment form and a lot of manual typing. Fleet Manager Pro puts all of it in one place, driven by what actually happens in the game. You drive — it does the paperwork.

It installs on your own Windows PC, opens in your web browser, and connects to American Truck Simulator and Euro Truck Simulator 2 through the game's official telemetry plugin. Your company's data stays on your computer, and your drivers can join from their own PCs over the network.

Live from the game

Every gauge, every job, every mile — as it happens.

Fleet Manager Pro plugs straight into the game's telemetry. No typing in job details, no screenshots of the delivery screen. The moment you start the engine, it knows.

Live Cockpit

Your truck's dashboard on a second screen

The Live Cockpit turns a second monitor, a laptop or a tablet into an extra dashboard for your truck. It refreshes four times every second, so the needles move with the game, not behind it.

At the top you see what matters on the road right now: speed and the current speed limit, engine RPM, the gear you're in, whether cruise control is on and how fast it's set. Below that is your current job — where you picked it up, where it's going, what you're hauling, how heavy it is, what it pays, how far is left and how long until the deadline.

The cockpit also watches the truck itself. Fuel and AdBlue levels, oil and water temperatures, oil and air pressures and battery voltage are all shown, with warnings when something goes out of range. Damage is broken down into engine, transmission, cabin, chassis, wheels and cargo, so you know exactly what that curb cost you.

  • Speed, speed limit, RPM, gear and cruise control
  • Current job with income, weight, deadline and distance left
  • Fuel, AdBlue, temperatures, pressures and battery
  • Damage per part — engine, transmission, cabin, chassis, wheels, cargo
  • Your steering, throttle, brake and clutch inputs
  • A transparent stream overlay you can add to OBS as a browser source
Streamers: the overlay shows your live speed, job and route on top of your stream without any extra software — just paste the overlay address into OBS.
Fleet Map

A real map, drawn by your drivers

The Fleet Map shows the real United States and Europe with true state and country borders and named cities, laid out in the same map projection the games use — so a position in the game lands in the right place on the map.

The clever part is the roads. Fleet Manager Pro doesn't ship a road map — your company draws it. Every road your drivers drive is recorded and remembered, so over time the map fills in with the routes your company actually runs. A busy corridor stands out at a glance; a state nobody has visited stays empty, waiting to be explored.

Every city you pick up from or deliver to is marked at its exact in-game spot, and every driver who is on the road right now appears live with their position and heading.

  • Live position of every driver in your company
  • Roads learned from real driving and kept forever
  • Pickup and delivery cities marked exactly
  • Separate American and European maps
  • Pan, zoom and switch games with one click
Telemetry Data

All 3,546 data points, one search away

For the curious — and for the people who like to know exactly what their truck is doing — the Telemetry Data page shows every single value the game publishes. That's 3,546 of them, covering the truck, drivetrain, lights, wheels, damage, the job, navigation, trailers, the game clock and more.

Values are grouped and searchable. Type "brake" and see every brake value; pick the "Trailers" group and see what's hitched behind you. You can download a full snapshot as a file to share or compare later.

Drivers who aren't on the same PC as the app run a small driver agent that sends their telemetry to the company over the network — so remote drivers show up live, and their deliveries log automatically too.

  • Every value from the game's telemetry plugin, live
  • Filter by name or by group
  • Download a snapshot as JSON
  • Remote drivers report in with the driver agent
Automatic Logbook

Every delivery, logged the moment you deliver it

The logbook is the heart of the company. When you take a job, Fleet Manager Pro starts tracking it. When you deliver, it writes the whole trip down for you: the route, cargo, weight, distance driven, what the job paid, fuel used, tolls, fines, cargo damage, your driving score and the XP you earned.

If the game knocked money off for damage or a late arrival, that deduction is shown and booked too, so the logbook matches what really happened. Open any job to see its full detail page, including the trail of the route you drove.

Because pay comes from real driving, Fleet Manager Pro also guards against cheating. Teleports, impossible speeds and odometer tricks are spotted, and the pay for that job is held until a manager reviews it.

Did a job before the app was running, or drive somewhere telemetry couldn't reach? Drivers can log a job by hand, and a manager approves it before it counts.

  • Route, cargo, weight, distance, pay, fuel, tolls and fines
  • Cargo damage and late deductions shown and booked
  • Driving score and XP on every delivery
  • Anti-cheat holds suspicious pay for a manager
  • Manual jobs with manager approval
  • Export the logbook to a spreadsheet (CSV)
Dispatch

Post a contract. Find it waiting in the game.

Most company tools let you write a job on a board and hope a driver finds something similar in the game. Fleet Manager Pro goes further: it can put the contract into the game's own freight market.

Contracts that match the real game

When you post a contract, you choose the pickup city, the destination, the company and the cargo from the game's own lists — every city, company and cargo in both American Truck Simulator and Euro Truck Simulator 2. You'll only see companies that really exist in the city you picked, so there are no impossible jobs on your board.

Add a bonus, a deadline and a priority. When a driver makes a delivery that matches the contract, it completes itself — no one has to tick a box.

Send to game, in three steps

  1. Save your game, then press Send to game on the contract and choose the save to start from.
  2. Fleet Manager Pro makes a new save slot with your job added to the freight market. Your other saves are never touched.
  3. Load that save in the game, drive to the pickup company and take the job. Deliver it and the contract completes.
  • Every city, company and cargo from ATS and ETS2
  • Only companies that really exist in the chosen city
  • Bonuses, deadlines and priorities — urgent contracts become urgent jobs
  • Matching deliveries complete the contract automatically
  • Works in any game language
ELD & Compliance

Hours of Service, kept on in-game time.

For realism-minded companies, Fleet Manager Pro includes a full electronic logging device. It records your duty status as you drive, using the game's clock — so a long haul in the game counts as a long haul in your logbook.

A real electronic logbook

Choose US or EU rules and the ELD tracks the clocks that matter: how long you can still drive, how much of your duty window is left, when your next break is due and where you stand on your weekly cycle. Each game keeps its own logbook, so your American and European driving never get mixed up.

Drivers can file pre-trip and post-trip vehicle inspection reports (DVIR), noting defects before they become breakdowns. Roadside inspections and violations are recorded too, adding a little risk to running out of hours.

Dispatchers see who is legal to drive. If a driver is out of hours, they're held from new dispatches until they've rested — just like a real carrier.

  • US or EU Hours-of-Service rules
  • Drive, window, break and cycle clocks
  • Pre- and post-trip inspections (DVIR)
  • Roadside inspections and violations
  • Dispatch holds when a driver is out of hours
  • A separate logbook for each game
The business side

Trucks, garages, leases and every dollar.

A trucking company is a business. Fleet Manager Pro gives yours a proper set of books — with prices taken from the games themselves.

Fleet & Garages

Your fleet, priced like the game

You don't have to type in your trucks. The first time a truck is driven, Fleet Manager Pro adds it to the fleet with its make, model and plate. From then on it tracks wear, logs services and repairs, and shows what each truck has earned and cost.

Buying is just as realistic. The dealer price list covers every stock truck and trailer in both games, and garages can be bought in any garage location the games offer. Garages carry weekly upkeep, and you can upgrade, edit or sell them later.

Short on cash? Lease a truck instead. Compare four lease plans side by side — each with a different length, mileage allowance, down payment and interest rate — with every cost shown before you sign. Pay weekly, and at the end buy the truck, hand it back or renew.

  • Trucks added automatically the first time they're driven
  • Wear, services and repairs for every truck
  • Dealer prices for every stock truck and trailer
  • Every garage location in both games; buy, edit and sell
  • Ownable trailers, with every trailer type the games allow
  • Truck leasing with four plans and weekly payments
  • The company account can't be overspent
Finance

A full company ledger

Every delivery, wage, fuel stop, toll, fine, repair, garage payment and truck purchase lands in one company ledger. The Finance page shows your balance, money in against money out, and a cash-flow chart, so you can see whether the company is growing or bleeding money.

Need to expand faster? Take a bank loan and pay it back weekly. Driver pay is worked out from each delivery and booked automatically, so payroll is never a chore.

  • Company balance and income vs expenses chart
  • Freight income, driver pay and every cost by category
  • Bank loans with weekly repayments
  • Automatic payroll from real deliveries
Expenses

Know exactly where the money goes

The Expenses page lists every cost the company has paid, with search, filters by category, truck and driver, and running totals at the top. Spot the truck that keeps needing repairs or the route that keeps getting fined.

Add your own expenses too — insurance, permits, office costs, whatever your company role-plays — and edit or delete them later. Everything exports to a spreadsheet when you want to dig deeper.

  • Every cost in one place, with filters and totals
  • Add, edit and delete your own expenses

Miles driven, loads on time

Open any driver to see every load they've run with its margin, so you can reward your best drivers and help the ones who are struggling.
