New: drivers have their own bank accounts (My Bank), separate from the company account. Pay is deposited every Friday at noon, with a statement and pay slips. Please note: drivers now pay their own fines (game, Hours of Service and inspection fines) from their bank account. To have the company pay them as before, turn off 'Drivers pay their own fines' in Settings, Economy. New: CDL & Points. A drivers test of two in-game hours, a licence with 12 points, traffic tickets and suspension at zero points; one point comes back every real day. It is off until you turn it on in Settings, Compliance. Also: the driver page shows the driver's own connection line.
