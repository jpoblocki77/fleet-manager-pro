Safer money: a double click, or two managers pressing the same button, can no longer pay a job, pay off a loan, end a lease or install an update twice. Drivers now see revenue, profit and costs only on their own jobs (Dispatchers, Managers and the Owner still see everything), and the deliveries spreadsheet holds only their own. Job applicants must be 16 or older. A job's fuel cost uses the diesel price paid in that job's game.
