Fuel and AdBlue prices: every diesel and AdBlue fill-up now has a price and a cost, charged to the company. Diesel uses the game's own price for the state or country the truck is in (expect it to cost more than the old flat price). AdBlue uses a price you set, about 1.20 per litre (4.54 per gallon) to start with; 0 makes it free. Both are in Settings, Economy. The Fuel & AdBlue page shows what a fill-up costs where you are and lists every fill-up. Swapping trucks or loading a save no longer counts as a fill-up.
