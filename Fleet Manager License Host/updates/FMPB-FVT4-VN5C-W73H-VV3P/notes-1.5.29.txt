Fleet Manager Pro is now free for everyone. There is no licence key and no activation any more, and it works without internet. The first time it starts after this update it shows the licence agreement: tick I agree to carry on (once per PC). Settings, Licence is now Settings, About. Your online Apply page, Global Leaderboard entry and VTC Finder profile move over to your company's own online ID by themselves the next time they sync. From now on every copy gets its updates from the same public channel.
